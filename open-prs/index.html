Ranking & methodology How this works＋

Coverage. Every open PR in vllm-project/vllm-omni, including drafts and bots. Cursor pagination avoids the 1,000-result search limit. The contributor list includes accounts with at least one current open PR.

Ranking. Open PR count is the default. Equal values share a rank. Status, bot, and committer filters recompute ranks; username search preserves them. You can also sort by 14-day submissions or recent activity. Counts are not a quality score.

New · 14d. All PRs created by that author in a rolling 14-day window, including open, merged, and closed PRs. This column is unaffected by the Draft filter. Window: Loading….

Exclude committers. Filters Active Committers and Lead Maintainers from the official governance roster (— people). The roster refreshes with the data. CODEOWNERS and organization membership are not used as proxies.

PR details. The right panel contains current open PRs only. Open a title or number to see the original PR. CI results and reviews are not collected.

Refresh. Automatic collection runs hourly; publishing can be delayed. Refresh data loads the latest published snapshot. Failed collections preserve the last complete snapshot. Times use your local timezone.
